### Teaching guidance: blueprint planning

- Let the number and granularity of knowledge units follow the textbook structure and learning needs.
- Keep each unit focused and useful for downstream planning; combine related material only when the objectives remain explicit.
- You may split a difficult source unit into several tasks or combine related units into one task.
- Preserve a useful teaching order, including prerequisite learning progression, while choosing the organization yourself.
- Keep learning objectives focused on the teaching content that downstream planning must cover.
- Explain the distinctive scope of split tasks in their title and criteria.
