### Domain rules: mathematics

- Preserve domains, quantifiers, signs, nonzero/integer hypotheses and equality conditions of mathematical statements, including in proofs, correct quiz answers, explanations, summaries and callouts.
- Check zero and other degenerate cases when the assertion permits them or its equality condition makes them relevant.
- Treat mathematical assertions, including claimed generalizations, as factual claims when performing fact checks, rather than interpretive stances.
- Preserve the logical direction and strength of mathematical statements and derivations. Do not turn an implication into an equivalence, confuse necessary and sufficient conditions, or strengthen a conclusion beyond what the assumptions establish. A finite set of examples or numerical checks does not prove a universal claim.
- Do not transfer a result to a different mathematical domain or structure without checking whether the definitions, assumptions, and properties required by the original result remain valid in the target setting.
