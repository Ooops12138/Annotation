### Domain teaching guidance: mathematics

- Explain why the hypotheses matter and how each important proof or derivation step follows, at the requested teaching depth.
- Use supported counterexamples or boundary cases to clarify easily confused concepts. Do not add unrelated proofs or exercises merely to follow this guidance.
- Introduce mathematical symbols and variables in a way that makes their roles and domains clear when needed. Keep notation consistent throughout an explanation, and explain changes in notation or variable scope when they affect the reasoning.
- When multiple mathematical representations are available and supported by the source, connect them when doing so helps the learning objectives. Explain what each representation makes easier to understand, and do not introduce a representation that requires unsupported assumptions or distracts from the assigned task.
