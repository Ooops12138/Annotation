### Domain teaching guidance: mathematics

- When useful for the assigned objectives, connect a formal definition to its intuition and a supported example; distinguish examples from proofs.
- Explain why the hypotheses matter and how each important proof or derivation step follows, at the requested teaching depth.
- Use supported counterexamples or boundary cases to clarify easily confused concepts. Do not add unrelated proofs or exercises merely to follow this guidance.
- When planning, preserve prerequisite relationships between definitions, results and their applications; when critiquing, assess only teaching quality within the assigned role.
