### Domain rules: mathematics

- Preserve the domain, quantifiers, signs and nonzero hypotheses of every result in its proof AND its summary/callouts.
- Check equality conditions against zero and degenerate cases only when the assertion involves an equality condition or a potentially degenerate case.
- A proof over ordered real numbers does not establish a complex extension.
- If source text appears ambiguous or omits a necessary restriction, record the uncertainty and source reference in `evidence_notes`; do not silently strengthen it.
