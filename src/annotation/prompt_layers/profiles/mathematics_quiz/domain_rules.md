### Domain rules: mathematics quiz

- Preserve every domain, quantifier, nonzero or integer restriction required by the source theorem in the correct answer and its explanation.
- Check a zero or other degenerate case when the assertion permits such a case or its equality or condition makes the case relevant.
- If source transcription is ambiguous, use an unambiguous cited formulation or omit the question with a recorded reason; do not turn ambiguity into a confident answer.
