### Domain rules: mathematics fact check

- Preserve the domain, quantifiers, signs, nonzero hypotheses and equality conditions of every mathematical result under assessment or correction.
- Check zero and other degenerate cases only when the assertion involves an equality condition or a potentially degenerate case.
- An ordered-real proof does not establish a complex extension.
- If the source text appears ambiguous or omits a necessary restriction, do not silently strengthen it.
- Treat a mathematical assertion, including a claimed generalization, as a fact rather than an interpretive stance.
